# Feature Engineering Documentation  
## Customer Churn Prediction Project

---

## 1. Introduction

Feature engineering is a critical step in machine learning that involves creating new features from existing data to improve model performance and interpretability.

In this project, feature engineering was performed to:
- Capture customer behavior more effectively
- Translate business understanding into numerical signals
- Improve the predictive capability of the churn prediction model

---

## 2. Importance of Feature Engineering

Raw datasets often do not contain sufficient information for machine learning models to learn complex patterns.

Feature engineering helps to:
- Highlight meaningful relationships
- Reduce noise in raw variables
- Improve accuracy and generalization of the model
- Incorporate domain knowledge into the model

---

## 3. Original Features Used

Before feature engineering, the following key numerical features were available:

- Tenure
- MonthlyCharges
- TotalCharges
- SeniorCitizen
- Encoded categorical variables

While useful, these features alone do not fully describe customer engagement and value.

---

## 4. Engineered Features (5+ Features)

The following new features were created based on business logic and statistical reasoning.

---

### 4.1 Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

**Formula:**

CLV = MonthlyCharges × Tenure

**Purpose:**
- Represents total revenue generated by a customer
- High CLV customers are less likely to churn

**Impact:**
- Helps the model distinguish high-value loyal customers from low-engagement users

---

### 4.2 Long_Tenure Indicator

**Definition:**

Long_Tenure = 1 if Tenure > 12 months else 0


**Purpose:**
- Identifies customers with long service duration
- Long-term customers generally show higher retention

**Impact:**
- Improves churn classification by separating stable customers from new users

---

### 4.3 High_Spender Indicator

**Definition:**

High_Spender = 1 if MonthlyCharges > average MonthlyCharges else 0


**Purpose:**
- Identifies customers who pay above-average charges
- High spenders may have different churn behavior

**Impact:**
- Helps the model learn pricing-related churn patterns

---

### 4.4 Average Monthly Cost

**Formula:**

Average_Monthly_Cost = TotalCharges / (Tenure + 1)

**Purpose:**
- Normalizes total charges across customer lifespan
- Prevents division by zero

**Impact:**
- Provides a stabilized measure of customer spending behavior

---

### 4.5 Engagement Score

**Formula:**

Engagement_Score = (0.6 × Tenure) + (0.4 × MonthlyCharges)


**Purpose:**
- Combines usage duration and payment level
- Represents overall customer engagement

**Impact:**
- Improves model sensitivity to behavioral patterns

---

## 5. Feature Scaling Considerations

Engineered features were scaled using:
- **StandardScaler** for model compatibility
- **Min-Max Scaling** for comparative analysis

Scaling ensures:
- Fair contribution of all features
- Faster model convergence
- Improved performance for distance-based computations

---

## 6. Feature Selection Rationale

Feature selection was performed using:
- Correlation analysis
- Feature importance from Random Forest

Features with higher relevance to churn were retained to:
- Reduce dimensionality
- Improve generalization
- Avoid multicollinearity

---

## 7. Validation of Engineered Features

Engineered features were validated by:
- Inspecting feature distributions
- Checking correlation with churn
- Evaluating impact on model accuracy

The inclusion of engineered features improved interpretability and prediction confidence.

---

## 8. Business Interpretation

From a business perspective:
- High CLV and long-tenure customers show lower churn risk
- High monthly charges without long tenure indicate higher churn probability
- Engagement score effectively summarizes customer behavior

These insights can help businesses design retention strategies.

---

## 9. Final Summary

Feature engineering significantly enhanced the dataset by transforming raw attributes into meaningful indicators of customer behavior and value.

The engineered features:
- Improved predictive accuracy
- Added business relevance
- Supported robust churn classification

This approach follows industry-standard machine learning practices and ensures a strong foundation for churn prediction.
