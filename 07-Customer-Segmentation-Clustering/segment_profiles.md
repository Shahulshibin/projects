# Customer Segment Profiles

## 1. Premium Spenders
**Description:**  
Customers in this segment have high monthly and total charges with long tenure.  
They are less price-sensitive and value service quality.

**Key Characteristics:**
- High Monthly Charges
- Long Tenure
- Low churn probability

**Business Importance:**  
This segment contributes the highest revenue and must be retained through loyalty programs.

---

## 2. Budget Conscious
**Description:**  
Customers who prefer low-cost plans and show high sensitivity to price changes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Low Monthly Charges
- Short to medium tenure
- High churn risk

**Business Importance:**  
Targeted discounts and affordable plans can reduce churn in this segment.

---

## 3. Young Professionals
**Description:**  
Customers with moderate spending and preference for flexible, digital payment methods.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Medium Monthly Charges
- Paperless billing users
- Moderate churn probability

**Business Importance:**  
Digital engagement and app-based offers work best for this segment.
